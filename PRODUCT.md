## Platform

## Users

Visitors looking for an illustrated bookmark to accompany their reading.

## Product Purpose

Present the provided book-mark designs and photographs so visitors can explore them clearly.

## Positioning

The site showcases illustrated bookmark designs through their real supplied photographs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The original archive contains 13 photographs in four design groups; the additional archive contains 78 individually coded bookmark photographs.
- Visitors can search the additional photographs by their printed filename code.
- The displayed price is €4.50 per bookmark; shipping is €2.50 per order for one to four bookmarks and free from five.
- Orders are received via WhatsApp or email. The verified WhatsApp contact is +32 465 75 82 40 and the email address is boekenleggers.be@gmail.com.
- Dimensions and material are not provided and must not be invented.

## Evidence on Hand

The 13 product photographs supplied in `assets/bookmarks/`, 78 photographs in `assets/bookmarks/catalog/`, and V2 pricing/contact details.

## Product Principles

- Let the supplied artwork lead.
- Make every photo easy to inspect.
- Keep product facts limited to what is known.
- Keep browsing responsive and keyboard accessible.
