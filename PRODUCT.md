# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors looking for an illustrated bookmark to accompany their reading.

## Product Purpose

Present the provided book-mark designs and photographs so visitors can explore them clearly.

## Positioning

The site showcases four illustrated bookmark designs through their real supplied photographs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The supplied archive contains 13 photographs in four design groups.
- Prices, ordering details, contact information, dimensions, and material are not provided and must not be invented.

## Evidence on Hand

The 13 product photographs supplied in `assets/bookmarks/`. No purchase or contact destination was supplied.

## Product Principles

- Let the supplied artwork lead.
- Make every photo easy to inspect.
- Keep product facts limited to what is known.
- Keep browsing responsive and keyboard accessible.
