# Design system

The website is an image-led Turkish showcase for the provided illustrated bookmarks. The art spans coastal florals, a playful character, a winter animal, and a holographic flower; the interface gives those photos a contrasting, lively frame without adding product claims.

## Visual world

- Deep ink teal (`#123f43`, `#0c3034`) anchors the opening and close.
- Citrus lime (`#d7ed76`) marks navigation and actions; coral (`#ff765e`) punctuates the page.
- Pale book-paper (`#fbf8ee`) keeps product photography legible.
- Outfit display faces pair with DM Sans body copy, both with system fallbacks.
- Tall, cropped photo windows and offset framing echo bookmarks and handmade paper displays.

## Interaction

- The primary action scrolls to the design collection.
- Each image opens a keyboard-operable photo viewer with previous/next controls and Escape support.
- The opening showcase turns through all 78 catalog photos from right to left; arrows, trackpad gestures, dragging, and keyboard arrows move between bookmarks.
- The full 78-photo catalog can be searched by image code.
- Motion is brief and optional under reduced-motion preferences.
- Layout adapts from five-column catalog browsing to two-column mobile.
